# TradeCraft — Trading Philosophy

> Version: 1.0.0 | Status: APPROVED | Last Updated: 2026-07-28

## Core Belief

The market is uncertain. Our edge, if any, comes from disciplined risk management, systematic process, and patient execution — not from prediction confidence.

## Priority Hierarchy

This ordering is absolute. A lower-priority goal may NEVER override a higher-priority goal.

1. **Legal and regulatory compliance** — We operate within the law.
2. **Capital preservation** — Protecting what we have comes before growing it.
3. **Prevention of catastrophic loss** — No single event may threaten the portfolio's survival.
4. **Data correctness** — Decisions built on bad data are bad decisions.
5. **Risk management** — Quantified, enforced, never bypassed.
6. **Strategy robustness** — Strategies must work across conditions, not just in backtests.
7. **Execution correctness** — Orders must do what we intend.
8. **Long-term risk-adjusted returns** — Risk-adjusted performance matters more than raw return.
9. **Raw return** — Profit is the goal, but the last priority.

## Cash Is a Position

The system must be comfortable holding cash. Being fully invested is not a goal.

**NO TRADE is a valid and desirable decision** when:
- No strategy identifies a sufficiently attractive opportunity
- Risk conditions are elevated
- Market regime is unfavourable
- Portfolio is at or near risk limits
- Data quality is insufficient

Never manufacture trades to satisfy arbitrary activity targets.

## No Profit Targets

There is NO required monthly, weekly, or daily profit target. Setting such targets creates pressure to take suboptimal trades. Profit is a consequence of good process, not a target to be forced.

## Reinvestment

Profit should initially be fully reinvested into the portfolio. There is no withdrawal schedule. The portfolio compounds.

## Holding Period

Swing trading is the primary style, but we do NOT impose an arbitrary fixed holding duration. Positions may be held:

- Days — typical swing trades
- Weeks — when trend continuation is validated
- Months — when justified by validated trailing rules

The exit decision comes from strategy rules and risk management, not from calendar pressure.

## Systematic Process

Every trading decision must be traceable to a defined, validated, and version-controlled process. Intuition and LLM opinions are inputs to research, never inputs to execution.

## Humility

- We do not know the future
- We do not assume a strategy that worked will continue working
- We do not assume a popular technique has an edge
- We do not assume data is correct without verification
- We do not assume regulation hasn't changed
- We acknowledge that losses are a normal part of trading

## Relationship with AI

AI is a powerful research assistant. AI is NOT:
- An oracle
- A regulatory authority
- A source of verified data
- An execution controller
- A risk manager

AI proposes. Humans approve. Deterministic code executes.
