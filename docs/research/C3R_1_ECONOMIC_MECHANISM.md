# C3R_1 — ECONOMIC MECHANISM & COUNTER-EVIDENCE ANALYSIS (ALPHA-015)

> **AUDIT FOCUS**: Institutional microfoundations, behavioral drivers, and Section A.5 failure modes analysis

---

## 1. ECONOMIC MICROFOUNDATIONS
- **Institutional Execution Constraints**: Large mutual funds and FIIs cannot enter a 5% position in a NIFTY 50 stock in a single day without incurring massive market impact. Orders are sliced over weeks, causing price continuation.
- **Benchmark Tracking Allocations**: Capital flows into top-performing equity funds, forcing fund managers to buy benchmark sector leaders.

---

## 2. SECTION A.5 — COUNTER-EVIDENCE & FAILURE MODES
- **Momentum Crashes**: Panic market recoveries cause high-beta distressed stocks to surge while momentum leaders lag.
- **Choppy Sideways Markets**: Whipsaws erode capital during non-trending periods.
- **India NIFTY Concentration**: Reliance, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Infosys, and TCS dominate index weighting, requiring robust relative strength normalization.
