# HYPOTHESIS DISCOVERY FRAMEWORK & ADMISSION PROCESS

Every research hypothesis proposed for future research cycles MUST undergo formal admission evaluation before any backtest execution.

---

## 1. MANDATORY ADMISSION CHECKLIST (8 POINTS)

1. **Economic Rationale**: Clear macro/micro explanation of economic drivers.
2. **Behavioral Rationale**: Documented investor psychological bias.
3. **Literature Support**: Academic or practitioner research citations.
4. **Novelty Verification**: Automatic novelty score $\ge 0.65$ vs Research Graveyard lineages.
5. **Point-in-Time Implementability**: 100% executable without lookahead bias.
6. **Transaction Cost Awareness**: Cost sensitivity analysis accounted for under Indian friction models.
7. **Suitable for Indian Equities**: Compatible with NIFTY 50 / 100 / 200 / 250 / 500 liquid equity universes.
8. **Explicit Falsification Criteria**: Pre-declared quantitative bounds (e.g. Expectancy R $< 0.25$R or Profit Factor $< 1.30$).

---

## 2. GRAVEYARD LINEAGE PROTECTION

Hypotheses exhibiting keyword similarity $> 0.35$ with abandoned lineages (`strat_trend_pullback`, `strat_momentum_rs`, `strat_breakout_confirm`, `strat_mean_reversion`) are **AUTOMATICALLY REJECTED**.
