# ADR-004: WHY THE IMMUTABLE RESEARCH GRAVEYARD IS MANDATORY

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Quantitative research organizations frequently waste capital and time re-testing failed ideas under slightly altered names or parameters when institutional memory is lost.

## Decision
All strategy lineages rejected during Development research are permanently locked in the `RESEARCH_GRAVEYARD`. A rejected strategy family cannot be revived, re-parameterized, or re-tested unless a future proposal first proves a materially different economic hypothesis.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Prevents cyclic data-mining and disguised retries of failed strategies.
- **Positive**: Preserves negative evidence as a permanent institutional asset.
