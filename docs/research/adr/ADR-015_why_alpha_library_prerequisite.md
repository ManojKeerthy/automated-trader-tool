# ADR-015: WHY THE ALPHA LIBRARY IS A MANDATORY PREREQUISITE FOR RESEARCH CYCLE 2

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Inventing strategy ideas without grounded economic and behavioral literature foundation results in empirical curve-fitting and random parameter searches.

## Decision
Future research hypotheses MUST originate from one of the 20 pre-registered alpha sources in `AlphaLibrary`. Unanchored hypotheses are strictly prohibited.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: High scientific rigor and strong economic grounding for all future strategies.
