## 1. COMPREHENSIVE ACADEMIC CITATION MATRIX

1. **Jegadeesh & Titman (1993)** — *Journal of Finance*: Analyzed CRSP US equities 1965–1989. Proved 3–12 month price momentum generates +1.0%/month excess return.
2. **Moskowitz & Grinblatt (1999)** — *Journal of Finance*: Analyzed US industry portfolios 1963–1995. Proved industry/sector momentum explains a substantial fraction of stock-level momentum.
3. **Jegadeesh & Titman (2001)** — *Journal of Finance*: Evaluated out-of-sample 1990–1998 US equities. Confirmed persistence of momentum excess returns, disproving data-mining critiques.
4. **Antonacci (2014)** — *Journal of Portfolio Management*: Introduced Dual Momentum (combining cross-sectional relative momentum with time-series absolute momentum) across global equities, commodities, and bonds, significantly reducing max drawdowns.
5. **Daniel & Moskowitz (2016)** — *Journal of Financial Economics*: Analyzed US equities 1927–2013. Identified momentum crash dynamics during sudden market rebounds following panic sell-offs.
