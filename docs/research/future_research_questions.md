# UNANSWERED RESEARCH QUESTIONS FOR FUTURE CYCLES

> **RESEARCH QUESTION BANK**: Open questions formulated during Research Cycle 1 for prospective investigation in Research Cycle 2 (NIFTY 250 universe).

---

> [!NOTE]
> None of these questions have been backtested or evaluated. They represent theoretical and empirical research directions for future pre-registration.

1. **Cross-Sectional Momentum & Relative Strength**: Does ranking NIFTY 250 constituents by 63-day relative strength generate stronger continuation edge when combined with a market-breadth filter (e.g., $\% \text{ Nifty 250 stocks} > \text{SMA50}$)?
2. **Volatile Squeeze Expansion**: Does filtering breakouts by historical 20-day ATR percentile ($\text{ATR}(14) / \text{SMA}(\text{Close}, 50) \le \text{25th percentile}$) isolate true volatility contractions across a broader 250-stock universe?
3. **Liquidity Impact & Volume Disparity**: How does execution friction scale when expanding from liquid NIFTY 50 large-caps to mid-cap NIFTY 250 equities?
4. **Intraday vs Overnight Return Decomposition**: What proportion of NIFTY 250 trend persistence originates from overnight gaps versus intraday session trends?
5. **Sector Relative Strength Disparity**: Does selecting top-performing stocks within top-performing industry sectors improve win rate and expectancy over universe-wide ranking?
